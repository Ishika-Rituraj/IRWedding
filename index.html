Time9:15 AM
VenueVia Lakhela Lakeview Resort & Spa
Kumbhalgarh, Rajasthan
NoteA beautiful Rajasthani tradition where the maternal family blesses the bride or groom with gifts and love. A deeply heartfelt ceremony.

VenueGrand Saffron Chamber
Via Lakhela Lakeview Resort & Spa
Kumbhalgarh, Rajasthan

Getting Here

Nearest Airports & Road Distances

Maharana Pratap Airport, Udaipur (UDR) — ~100 km, approx. 1 hr 45 min by road. Well connected from Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, and more. Ahmedabad Airport (AMD) — ~220 km, approx. 3.5 hours by road. Flights from most major cities. Jaipur Airport (JAI) — ~340 km, approx. 4.5 hours by road. Well connected nationally and internationally. Road distances from key cities Udaipur → Kumbhalgarh: ~2 hours  ·  Jaipur: ~3.5 hours  ·  Ahmedabad: ~3.5 hours

Nearby Attractions

Kumbhalgarh Fort is just 3.3 km away — a UNESCO World Heritage Site with the world's second-longest wall. The Kumbhalgarh Wildlife Sanctuary is steps from the resort. Ranakpur Jain Temple and Udaipur's City Palace are both within easy reach.
